**Carere** (hereinafter referred to as "the Company") has established the following privacy policy. By building a framework for the protection of personal information and ensuring that all employees recognize the importance of protecting personal information and take appropriate action, the Company is committed to safeguarding personal information.

## Personal Information We Collect

The Company collects the following personal information.

### Contact Form

- Name
- Phonetic name
- Company name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Inquiry details

This information is used solely for the purpose of responding to inquiries and contacting you. It will not be disclosed to third parties without your consent.

## Management of Personal Information

The Company keeps customers' personal information accurate and up to date, and takes necessary measures such as maintaining security systems, establishing management structures, and thoroughly educating employees to prevent unauthorized access, loss, damage, alteration, or leakage of personal information. We implement security measures and strictly manage personal information.

## Purpose of Use of Personal Information

Personal information entrusted to us by customers will be used for communications from the Company, business notifications, responses to inquiries, and the sending of emails and materials.

## Prohibition of Disclosure and Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Company properly manages personal information entrusted to us by customers and will not disclose personal information to third parties except in the following cases.

- When the customer has given consent
- When disclosure is made to a service provider to whom the Company outsources work in order to provide services requested by the customer
- When disclosure is required by law

## Security Measures for Personal Information

The Company takes thorough security measures to ensure the accuracy and safety of personal information.

## Inquiries by the Individual

If a customer wishes to inquire about, correct, or delete their personal information, we will respond after verifying the identity of the individual.

## Compliance with Laws and Regulations and Policy Review

The Company complies with French laws, regulations, and other standards applicable to the personal information it holds, and will review and improve the content of this policy as appropriate.
